    What this is

    Before You Pay turns public rules into plain steps: the IRS rules for nonprofit hospital financial assistance (Section 501(r)), the No Surprises Act and its Good Faith Estimate dispute, your HIPAA right to your billing records, Texas law on itemized bills, state charity-care laws, and the CFPB's debt dispute guidance. It doesn't store, send or see anything you enter. It's self-help information, not legal or financial advice. For a large or complex bill, talk to a patient advocate or an attorney.
